The Sacred Map

The Path of Progression & Initiation

This is the path walked by the greatest minds and leaders in human history — Einstein, Tesla, Da Vinci, Newton, Churchill. A sacred progression of initiation, healing, and self-mastery within the Royal Lineage of King Salomon — awakening your divine potential, clearing the negative ego, and connecting you to the guidance of your Higher Self. Each step builds upon the last. Each initiation opens a new level of Light.

I
The Awakening
Life Activation

The journey begins here. The Life Activation is a powerful 1-on-1 healing session that awakens your physical and spiritual DNA, reconnecting you to your original divine blueprint and your life's true purpose. It restores and balances your whole energy system, amplifying your connection to your Higher Self and opening you to a greater flow of Light.

This is the essential first step on the Path of Progression in the Royal Lineage of King Salomon — the awakening that makes everything that follows possible.

II
First Step of Initiation
Empower Thyself

Initiation is a sacred ceremony that has been passed from teacher to student in the Mystery School Tradition for over 3,000 years — a direct transmission of Light that permanently elevates your energetic reality and your capacity to live from your Higher Self.

The Empower Thyself 2-day program is your formal entry into the lineage. You receive ancient tools for working with your energy field, protecting yourself from negative influences, mastering the negative ego, and living with greater clarity, power, and purpose. You step into the Royal Lineage of King Salomon — joining a global family of initiates committed to the Great Work of serving the Light.

This is not a seminar. It is not a workshop. It is a sacred rite of passage that changes you at the level of the soul.

III
2nd Step of Initiation
Healers Academy

Become a Healer — heal yourself and help others to heal too. When you have received the Light, it is natural to want to share it with others.

Here you learn how to work with your spiritual senses, further purifying and expanding your awareness and perception of self and the world around you. During this 5-day program, you will go through tremendous healing of yourself while being embraced in the highest healing energy — Agape Love.

As you heal at the core of your being, you learn how to administer the Life Activation, Full Energy Balancing, and Aura Healing, alongside mastering the art of guiding the transformative Max Meditation System.

After this Second Step Initiation, the deeper hermetic teachings of the lineage become available to you, should you wish to expand your knowledge and understanding.

IV
3rd Step of Initiation
Ritual Master

Become a Ritual Master by overcoming the negative ego.

At this level, you begin your training as a warrior of light — sharpening the arts of discernment, protection, and magick that will help you overcome the negative ego and create a better life. You step fully into the Great Work, serving the Light and serving humanity with mastery, commitment, and love.

The Ritual Master path is considered one of the most challenging paths to walk, as it requires deep commitment, discipline, and the humility needed to continuously overcome the ego. It is not a destination — it is a way of life.

Those who walk this path are among the most dedicated servants of the Light in the world today.

The Great Work

Walking the Path of Self-Mastery

All Mystery Schools across time agree: to achieve enlightenment and self-mastery, one must know oneself fully. Walking this sacred path, you explore deeper aspects of self — tapping into your full potential, reconnecting to your true divine expression, and transcending the challenges and obstacles of life with more ease and grace.

As you let go of the illusion of the lower self, the negative ego, and the limitations of survival mode, you tap into your true strength, empowerment, and inner wisdom. You begin to live from your Higher Self — guided, purposeful, and free.

When we live in alignment with our life's true purpose, we experience a deep sense of inner peace, joy, and true freedom — living every day with pure joy, true freedom, inner peace, and abundance.

In the Modern Mystery School tradition, to Know Thyself is not solely the inward journey of self-realisation and empowerment. It is most importantly the outward expression of love and service, compassion, and empowerment for all.

Serve the Light

Your Healing Heals the World

If you are called to help others, know that your healing — each step you take toward your own enlightenment — can heal the world. Your transformation causes a ripple of great change in the world around you. This is the sacred purpose of the Mystery School Tradition: a humanity living in joy, unity, and remembrance of its true divine nature.
